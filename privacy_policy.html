Privacy Policy for Gym Tracker

Effective Date: October 16, 2026

1. Overview

Thank you for using Gym Tracker ("the App"). Your privacy is important to us. Gym Tracker is a personal workout logging and fitness tracking application developed by grzechog. The App is designed with a privacy-first approach: all your workout logs, custom muscle segments, exercise history, weight, and rep logs are stored strictly locally on your device.

2. Information We Collect

Gym Tracker does NOT collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII) or user data to external servers or third parties.

  • Local Data Storage: All data created within the App (such as muscle segments, exercise names, logged sets, weights, reps, timestamps, and app theme settings) is saved strictly in an offline, local database on your device.
  • No Account Required: You do not need to create an account, log in, or provide any personal information to use the App.
  • No Third-Party Analytics: The App does not integrate any third-party tracking, advertising networks, or analytics SDKs.

3. Data Security & Control

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you have full control over your workout data at all times. You can delete individual sets, exercises, or entire muscle segments at any time directly in the App. Uninstalling the App or clearing app storage in your device settings will permanently remove all stored workout data.
