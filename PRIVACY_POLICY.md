# Privacy Policy for Gym Tracker

**Effective Date:** October 16, 2026  
**Last Updated:** October 16, 2026  

Thank you for using **Gym Tracker** ("the App"). Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy outlines how **Gym Tracker** handles your information.

---

## 1. Overview
Gym Tracker is a personal workout logging and fitness tracking application developed by **grzechog**. The App is designed with a **privacy-first approach**: all your workout logs, custom muscle segments, exercise history, weight, and rep logs are stored **strictly locally** on your device.

---

## 2. Information We Collect
**Gym Tracker does NOT collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII) or user data to external servers or third parties.**

* **Local Data Storage:** All data created within the App (such as muscle segments, exercise names, logged sets, weights, reps, timestamps, and app theme settings) is saved strictly in an offline, local SQLite/Room database on your device.
* **No Account Required:** You do not need to create an account, log in, or provide an email address or phone number to use the App.
* **No Third-Party Analytics:** The App does not integrate any third-party tracking, advertising networks, or analytics SDKs that track user behavior across apps or websites.

---

## 3. Permissions
The App uses minimal standard system permissions required strictly for core app functionality and Android backup/restore features:
* **Storage / Backup Rules:** Allows the Android system's built-in backup and restore mechanism (`backup_rules.xml`) to transfer your local workout database when you switch or restore Android devices.

---

## 4. Data Security & Control
* **Your Control:** Since all data is stored locally on your device, you have full control over your workout data at all times.
* **Data Deletion:** You can delete individual sets, exercises, or entire muscle segments at any time directly in the App. Uninstalling the App or clearing app storage in your Android device settings will permanently remove all stored workout data.

---

## 5. Children's Privacy
The App does not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in certain jurisdictions). The App is safe for general audiences as it operates entirely offline and collects no personal data.

---

## 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this policy. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
